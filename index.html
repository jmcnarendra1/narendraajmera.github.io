About

Narendra Ajmera has spent over three decades in public engineering service in Rajasthan, working across infrastructure planning, municipal works, and civic project delivery. His career has centred on the practical machinery of government — how public works are tendered, executed, and held accountable.

In recent years his attention has turned toward the policy and legal architecture that governs public procurement: the rules, service codes, and accountability mechanisms that determine whether public money is well spent. He engages closely with the Rajasthan Transparency in Public Procurement Act, service conduct rules, and the Right to Information Act as instruments of everyday administrative accountability.
